    How to use this form, the rating scale and the assessment rules
    1. Complete the coach details and choose the tier being assessed. Only that tier is shown.
    2. Work through the numbered sections. Teaching and coaching are split into sub-sections.
    3. Choose a rating from 0 to 4 for every criterion from the drop-down list, then record the evidence or reference behind it.
    4. Choose the evidence methods used for each sub-section from the drop-down list. Not every method is needed for every criterion.
    5. Complete the summary and outcome at the end of the tier.

    Assessment rules
    • A tier is recognized only when every criterion is rated 3 or 4. Strengths in one area do not offset gaps in another.
    • Competency is verified through assessed evidence. Experience alone does not establish readiness.
    • Judge student progress from each student’s starting benchmark, not from raw results.
    • Send any borderline outcome to a second Director for review.
    • Give written feedback on every criterion rated below 3: what was missing and what to demonstrate next.
    • Recognizing a tier makes the coach eligible for promotion consideration. Passing does not guarantee promotion or a salary increase.
    The pathway at a glance

    The pathway starts at Tier 2. Tier 3 Coach is the shared foundation for two parallel directions: Tier 3 Lead and Tier 4 Specialist. Neither direction is an automatic next step.

    TIER 2: GOLF COACH
    Tier objective
    Apply foundational program knowledge, technical skills and coaching methods effectively with support.
    Expected experience
    1 to 5 years coaching experience
    2 years at IMG Academy

    Section 1: IMG Golf Program

    1Explain the Coaching Map and benchmarking philosophy.
    2Apply benchmarking to identify student needs.
    3Demonstrate useful Coach Hub navigation and application.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 2: Teaching

    Technical

    1Explain Ball and Club measures; collect accurate TrackMan data.
    2Complete Gears Levels 1 and 2 (theory and practical).
    3Complete Smart2Move Level 1 and putting Level 1 (SAM PuttLab or Capto).
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Practical

    1Deliver an observed lesson aligned with the Coaching Map.
    2Demonstrate GASP setup principles in practice.
    3Complete a Player Evaluation Profile assessment.
    4Design and explain a purposeful 4–5 station coaching circuit.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 3: Coaching

    On-course

    1Explain the purpose of essential on-course activities.
    2Demonstrate a learning-focused on-course activity.
    3Connect course statistics with practice priorities.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Performance

    1Explain sound practice-round preparation.
    2Design a facility session for student or group needs.
    3Justify activity selection and feedback methods.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 4: Coaching Development

    1Prepare a Golf Plan from benchmarks and course data.
    2Complete a clear, honest scorecard and session video.
    3Film two coaching moments and reflect on improvement.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    TIER 3: COACH
    Tier objective
    Independently deliver high-quality coaching and evidence meaningful student development and experience.
    Expected experience
    3 to 7 years coaching experience
    4 years at IMG Academy

    Section 1: IMG Golf Program

    1Independently apply Coaching Map, benchmarking and development philosophy.
    2Build and adapt plans using relevant student evidence.
    3Use Coach Hub to support analysis, planning and reporting.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 2: Teaching

    Technical

    1Complete Gears Level 3 and Smart2Move Levels 2 and 3 (theory and practical).
    2Integrate GASP, Ball, Club and body-motion evidence.
    3Use technology to identify improvement needs and prioritize justified solutions.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Practical

    1Deliver consistently effective individual and group sessions.
    2Assess students using an advanced Player Evaluation Profile.
    3Adapt teaching in real time to learning and performance needs.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 3: Coaching

    On-course

    1Design and deliver purposeful decision-making and scoring sessions.
    2Assess on-course performance against benchmarks.
    3Evaluate intervention results and adapt the plan.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Performance

    1Plan and evaluate effective practice sessions.
    2Create and review tournament preparation processes.
    3Use performance behaviors and outcomes to plan next steps.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 4: Coaching Development

    1Document student development and positive experience evidence.
    2Film and self-assess two sessions; receive Director feedback.
    3Present a coach education session on a chosen topic.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    TIER 3: LEAD
    Tier objective
    Guide and develop fellow coaches to improve coaching quality and student experience across a team.
    Expected experience
    7+ years coaching experience
    4 years at IMG Academy

    Section 1: IMG Golf Program

    1Explain IMG standards and how coaching methods align across a pod.
    2Identify inconsistencies and recommend evidence-based improvement.
    3Guide colleagues’ use of program benchmarking and planning.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 2: Teaching

    Technical

    1Clearly explain technology findings and priorities to colleagues.
    2Evaluate another coach’s technical reasoning and decisions.
    3Use constructive feedback to improve technical coaching judgment.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Practical

    1Observe and assess another coach’s session.
    2Give specific, actionable developmental feedback.
    3Adapt communication to grow capability and confidence.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 3: Coaching

    On-course

    1Evaluate another coach’s on-course learning design.
    2Guide improvements to course decision-making and scoring activities.
    3Assess how feedback changes coaching practice.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Performance

    1Educate coaches in purposeful facility practice design.
    2Guide use of performance data and tournament planning.
    3Demonstrate effective coaching-team problem solving.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 4: Coaching Development

    1Document a coach-development case study.
    2Deliver and evaluate a coach education workshop.
    3Demonstrate mentoring and supportive leadership through observed practice.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    TIER 4: SPECIALIST
    Tier objective
    Demonstrate deep expertise and exceptional teaching in a defined golf specialty, evidenced through student and coach development.
    Expected experience
    10+ years coaching experience
    7+ years at IMG Academy

    Section 1: IMG Golf Program

    1Explain how specialist expertise supports the IMG Coaching Map.
    2Identify where specialization benefits student development.
    3Present a credible improvement opportunity within the program.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 2: Specialist Expertise

    1Demonstrate advanced education and subject competence.
    2Collect, interpret and apply relevant specialty data.
    3Defend coaching decisions with evidence and player context.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 3: Teaching Application

    1Design and present a progressive specialist curriculum.
    2Demonstrate effective individual and group delivery.
    3Adapt teaching to ages, abilities and learning styles.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 4: Student Development

    1Identify specialist needs using sound assessment.
    2Present assessed student case studies with measurable outcomes.
    3Explain transfer to on-course play and broader student development.
    Evidence methods used: none selected

    Section 5: Coach Education

    1Deliver an assessed specialist education session.
    2Evaluate coach understanding and adapt education.
    3Present a scalable coach learning framework and constructive feedback.
    Evidence methods used: none selected
